# SMS Message Templates

Placeholders use `[brackets]`. Keep sender ID and links to your own verified domains — carrier filters flag shortened/unknown links more than any single word.

---

## 1. Transactional

**OTP / Verification**
`[Business]: Your verification code is [OTP]. It expires in [X] minutes. Do not share this code with anyone.`

**Order Confirmation**
`Hi [Name], your order #[OrderID] has been confirmed. Total: [Amount]. Expected delivery: [Date]. Track: [link]`

**Payment Confirmation**
`Payment received: [Amount] for [Item/Service] on [Date]. Ref: [TxnID]. Thank you for using [Business].`

**Payment Failed**
`Hi [Name], your payment of [Amount] for [Item] was not successful. Please retry or contact support: [contact]`

**Delivery / Shipping Update**
`Your order #[OrderID] is out for delivery and should arrive by [Time/Date]. Rider: [Name], [Phone].`

**Low Wallet Balance**
`Hi [Name], your [Business] wallet balance is [Amount]. Fund your wallet to continue enjoying uninterrupted service.`

**Appointment Reminder**
`Reminder: You have an appointment with [Business/Person] on [Date] at [Time]. Reply CANCEL to reschedule.`

**Password Reset**
`A password reset was requested for your [Business] account. Use code [OTP] to proceed. If this wasn't you, ignore this message.`

**Subscription Renewal**
`Hi [Name], your [Service] subscription renews on [Date] for [Amount]. No action needed if details are unchanged.`

## 2. Promotional

**Seasonal Greeting + Offer**
`Happy [Holiday], [Name]! [Business] has something special for you this season: [link]`

## Words to Avoid (Common Spam/Scam Triggers)
free, win / winner, cash, prize, guarantee(d), click here, act now, urgent, congratulations, claim now, 100% risk-free, no cost, limited time (used excessively), all caps text, excessive exclamation marks (!!!)

## Compliance Notes
- Include opt-out instructions (`Reply STOP`) on promotional messages.
- Use your registered sender ID; avoid unbranded shortened links where possible.
- Keep transactional messages factual and free of promotional language to protect deliverability.
